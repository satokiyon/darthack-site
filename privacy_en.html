DartHack ("the App") respects user privacy and operates in accordance with the following policy.

1. Information Collected

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect the following information:

  • Advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS, model, language settings, etc.)
  • Ad impressions and click data
  • Approximate location information (country level)

2. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used for ad delivery, optimization, fraud prevention, and performance measurement.

3. Provision to Third Parties

The App does not directly share user information with third parties. However, Google may use information for advertising purposes.

4. External Services

The App uses Google AdMob and Google Play.

5. Management of Personal Information

The developer does not collect or store users' personal information through the App.

6. Contact

For inquiries, please open an issue on GitHub:
https://github.com/satokiyon/DartHack/issues

7. Revisions

This policy may be revised as needed. Any updated policy will be posted on this page.
